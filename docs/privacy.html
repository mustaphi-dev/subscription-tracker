Privacy Policy

Wince · Last updated 6 November 2026

Wince helps you keep track of subscriptions, free trials, pay-later instalments and account balances, and warns you before money leaves your account. This policy explains what personal data we use, why, who we share it with and what your rights are under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Wince is operated by Mustapha Adams, trading as Wince, the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at hello@trywince.app.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you for anyone else.

Connecting a bank

Bank connections use Open Banking through regulated account information service providers: TrueLayer for UK banks and Enable Banking for banks in the EU. You log in at your own bank; we never see or store your banking password. You choose which accounts to share, and access is read-only: Wince cannot move money. Consent lasts up to 90 days, after which we ask you to renew it. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at your bank, and we then stop collecting new data from that account.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers to run Wince. Each processes data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions (data stored in London, UK).
  • TrueLayer and Enable Banking: Open Banking connections to your bank.
  • Anthropic: reading receipts and forwarded emails to extract trial and subscription details.
  • Cloudflare: receiving emails you forward to your Wince address and passing them to our servers. Emails are not stored by Cloudflare.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
  • logo.dev: showing merchant and bank logos. The app requests a logo using the merchant's name; no account details are sent.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, or ask us to, we delete your profile, accounts, items and bank data within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep by law. Disconnecting a bank stops new data being collected from it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each user's records are isolated by row-level security in the database. The app can be locked with a PIN or Face ID, and bank credentials never pass through our servers.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@trywince.app and we will reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Wince is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
